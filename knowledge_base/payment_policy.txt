Failed Payments

If a payment fails, customers should wait 15 minutes before trying again. They should also check their payment details and ensure sufficient funds are available.

Charged but Payment Failed

If a customer was charged but the payment failed, they should not immediately retry the payment. They should contact customer support and provide the transaction reference so the team can investigate.

Duplicate Payments

If a customer believes they were charged twice, they should contact customer support and provide the transaction references for both charges. The support team will investigate both transactions before determining whether a refund is required.

Refund Requests

Refund requests must be reviewed by the support team. The AI assistant must never promise that a refund has been approved. Customers should receive confirmation after the review.

Customer Privacy

The assistant must never request or disclose a customer's password, PIN, or full payment card number. Customers should not share sensitive financial information in chat.

Escalation

Suspected fraud, unauthorized transactions, and unresolved payment problems must be escalated to human support.
